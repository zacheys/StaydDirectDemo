Local Guide

Galveston at your doorstep.

Our favorite spots near the property.

The Spot

Multi-level restaurant on the Seawall with great burgers, seafood, and Gulf views from the rooftop.

5 min drive

Moody Gardens

Aquarium, rainforest pyramid, and IMAX theater. Great for families and rainy-day plans.

12 min drive

Pleasure Pier

Classic amusement pier with rides, games, and oceanfront dining. Walk the boardwalk at sunset.

8 min drive

Gaido's Seafood

A Galveston institution since 1911. Famous for fresh Gulf oysters and pecan-crusted snapper.

6 min drive

Stewart Beach

Family-friendly beach with lifeguards, restrooms, and chair rentals. Cleaner and less crowded.

3 min drive

The Strand Historic District

Victorian architecture, local shops, art galleries, and restaurants. Walkable and worth an afternoon.

10 min drive

Dolphin Tours

Bay cruises departing from Pier 21. Dolphin sightings practically guaranteed year-round.

10 min drive

La King's Confectionery

Old-school candy and ice cream shop on the Strand. Homemade saltwater taffy pulled fresh daily.

10 min drive
